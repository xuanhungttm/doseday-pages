DoseDay — Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

DoseDay is a pill reminder and medication tracking app. It is designed to work entirely on your device, with no account and no sign-up.

Data we collect

None. DoseDay does not collect, store on servers, sell, or share any personal data. We operate no backend servers for app data.

Data stored on your device

The profiles, medications, schedules, dose history and pill photos you enter are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage. They never leave your device except in reports you choose to export and share yourself (Doctor Report PDF/CSV). Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status; RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-generated identifier and purchase receipts from Apple — never your name, email, or medication data.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

DoseDay contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The app does not use your data for advertising and does not share data with data brokers.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push-notification server is involved.

Children

DoseDay is intended for adults managing their own or a family member's medications. It does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
