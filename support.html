Frequently asked questions

How do reminders work?
DoseDay schedules local notifications on your iPhone for each dose. Open the Today tab and tap Taken, Snooze (15 minutes) or Skip. You can also tap Taken or Snooze right on the notification. Make sure notifications are allowed for DoseDay in iOS Settings.
What is Refill Runway?
Enter how many pills you have on hand for a medication. DoseDay counts down as doses are confirmed and warns you — on the Meds tab and by notification — days before the med runs out. Refill Runway is a DoseDay Pro feature.
What is the Missed-Dose Safety Net?
With DoseDay Pro, a dose that isn't confirmed triggers a follow-up reminder after 15 minutes and again after 30 minutes. Missed doses also stay visible on the Today tab and in History until you act on them.
What is Senior Mode?
Senior Mode (Pro) switches the Today screen for that profile to extra-large type, high contrast and one giant Taken button — designed for a parent using their own phone. Turn it on in Settings for each profile.
How do I restore my subscription?
Open DoseDay → Settings → Restore Purchases. Your Pro status is tied to your Apple Account, so restoring works on any device signed in with the same account.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → DoseDay → Cancel Subscription.
Where is my data stored?
Only on your iPhone. DoseDay has no accounts and no cloud sync. Use History → Export to create a Doctor Report (PDF/CSV) you can share or print.

DoseDay is a reminder and tracking tool. It is not a medical device, does not provide medical advice, and does not check drug interactions. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions.
